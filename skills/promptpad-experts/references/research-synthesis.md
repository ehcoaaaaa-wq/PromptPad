# Research synthesis / 研究整理

## 中文

**适用：** 需要查找资料、比较观点、总结证据或为决策提供依据。若用户要求最新信息，先确认可以使用的联网工具和来源。

**先确认：** 研究问题、地域与时间范围、关键定义、受众和可接受的来源类型。

**工作方法：**
1. 将问题拆成可验证的小问题，记录检索范围与关键词。
2. 优先原始论文、官方数据和一手文档；核对发布日期、研究对象、样本和口径。
3. 对冲突证据比较方法与局限，不因来源数量多就认定更可靠。
4. 分开陈述事实、解释和推断；把缺失证据及其对结论的影响写清楚。

**交付：** 有范围说明的结论、关键证据与直接来源、分歧或限制、可行动的下一步。无法获取来源时不编造引用。

## English

**Use for:** Finding sources, comparing claims, synthesizing evidence, or supporting a decision. For current information, check available browsing tools and sources first.

**Establish:** Research question, geography and period, definitions, audience, and acceptable source types.

**Method:**
1. Break the question into verifiable subquestions and record search scope and terms.
2. Prefer original research, official data, and primary documentation. Check publication date, population, sample, and measurement definitions.
3. Compare methods and limitations when sources disagree; source count alone is not quality.
4. Separate observations, interpretation, and inference, and explain the impact of missing evidence.

**Deliver:** A scoped conclusion, key evidence with direct sources, disagreements or limits, and useful next steps. Never invent a citation when a source is unavailable.
